Payment Processing (Stripe Connect)

Payments run through Stripe Connect — the provider is paid their full agreed rate directly, and our 5% is collected automatically as a platform fee. Processing fees are real, and the payment rail you choose changes the margin dramatically:

RailFee covered byFamily paysStripe takesThe Village nets*
CardPlatform absorbs$31.50/hr~$147/mo$79/mo
CardFamily covers$32.42/hr~$152/mo$226/mo
ACH (bank)Family covers$31.63/hr~$20/mo$226/mo

Takeaway: on cards, the 2.9% + 30¢ fee eats most of the 5% service fee — absorbing it leaves only ~$79 per match. On ACH bank debit (0.8%, capped at $5 per charge) the fee is trivial. Use ACH and pass the small processing cost to the family: they pay ~$31.63/hr instead of $31.50, and The Village keeps essentially the full service fee.

*Net of Stripe processing and estimated Connect account fees, per match at $30/hr × 160 hrs/mo, billed weekly. These numbers are computed live in the app at /api/economics.
