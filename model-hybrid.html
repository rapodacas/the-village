Model C: Hybrid

Matching fee + memberships + a small service fee — three layers
Recommended

Three Revenue Layers

Because special-needs help is often lumpy (an advocate around IEP season, a tutor a few hours a week), the memberships carry the revenue and the service fee is a light topper — not the reverse.

Provider membership: $49/mo — waived for the first 90 days so they see the referrals before they pay.

How the money moves: The Village doesn't set rates or pay wages. The family and provider agree the rate (we suggest a range), and payments run through Stripe Connect — the provider is paid their full agreed rate directly, and our fee is collected automatically as a platform fee. Providers are independent contractors; The Village is a registry, not an employer.

How a Match Plays Out

Illustrative: an active match bills about $500/month of provider services (e.g., a tutor at ~$60/hr a couple times a week). Advocacy is lumpier; steady tutoring/coaching is higher — the memberships smooth it out.

Day 1: Match

Family pays the $299 matching fee. Roxy introduces the vetted provider. Support begins.

Month 1: Service Fee Only

Payments run through Stripe Connect. On ~$500/mo of billed services, the 5% fee ≈ $25/mo, collected automatically — the provider gets their full rate directly. Provider membership: waived (90-day grace).

Month 2+: Family Membership

Family membership begins: $99/mo + ~$25 service fee = ~$124/month from this one match.

Month 4+: Provider Pays Too

After 90 days, provider membership begins: $49/mo.
Total from this one match: ~$173/month (plus the one-time $299).

The memberships are the predictable base; matching fees are one-time cash injections on top. Conservatively building to 20–30 active matches over the first year lands this in the $40K–$70K/year range — a real business running alongside Roxy's teaching, not instead of it.

Payment Processing (Stripe Connect)

Payments run through Stripe Connect — the provider is paid directly, our 5% is auto-collected. At these smaller ticket sizes, the payment rail matters even more:

RailOn ~$500/mo billedStripe takesThe Village nets*
CardPlatform absorbs~$14.80/mo~$10/mo
ACH (bank)Family covers~$4/mo~$25/mo

Takeaway: a $500 card charge costs 2.9% + 30¢ ≈ $14.80 — which would eat most of the $25 service fee. ACH bank debit (0.8%, capped at $5) barely dents it. Use ACH and pass the tiny fee to the family. (Modeled live in the app at /api/economics.)
