The Problem

When a child has an IEP, a diagnosis, or just needs more than school provides, families are left to figure it out alone. They spend months hunting for an IEP advocate, a tutor who gets it, a social-skills group, or a break — through Facebook groups, waitlists, and word of mouth, with no way to know who's actually trustworthy.

And most families are paying out of pocket. Regional Center funds only a narrow slice (a qualifying diagnosis plus substantial disability in 3 of 7 life areas), so the majority of IEP and 504 families — learning disabilities, ADHD, speech delays — get no public funding and are on their own.

The Solution

The Village connects special-needs families with vetted, background-checked professionals — the help that schools and insurance don't give you — matched by someone who's spent her career in this world.

How It Works

  1. We vet every provider — DOJ/FBI Live Scan fingerprinting, California TrustLine registration, credential checks, and references. No one reaches a family until they've cleared it.
  2. We make the match — by your child's needs, personality, schedule, and goals. This is the heart of it: a real person who knows the difference between good help and the rest.
  3. You and the provider agree the terms directly — including the rate. The Village suggests a fair range but never sets it. Providers are independent professionals, not our employees.
  4. Payments run through Stripe Connect — you pay on the platform, the provider is paid their full agreed rate directly, and our small service fee is collected automatically. We never hold or pay out wages.
  5. The Village earns a 5% service fee plus a small monthly membership — a fraction of what fragmented, go-it-alone searching costs you in time and dead ends.

A registry, not an agency. The Village connects families with independent providers. We don't employ them, set their wages, or provide the services ourselves — and we don't give legal, medical, or therapeutic advice. Read our full disclosure.

Who We Connect You To

Vetted, independent, non-clinical special-education-adjacent professionals:

  • IEP / 504 Advocates
  • Special-Needs Tutors
  • Executive-Function Coaches
  • Parent & Behavior Coaches
  • Social-Skills Facilitators
  • Transition & Life-Skills Coaches
  • Respite Providers (coming soon)

We deliberately stay out of clinical, insurance-billed services (ABA, speech, OT, PT, therapy). The Village is private-pay and non-clinical — the trustworthy support around the medical and school systems, not a replacement for them.

Why The Village

Roxy Apodaca is a special-education teacher for children with autism — and a mother of five. She has spent her career sitting on both sides of the IEP table, walking families through a system that is confusing on its best day, and quietly keeping a mental list of the advocates, tutors, and coaches who are the real deal.

The Village is her way of handing that list to every family — with the trust, the vetting, and the heart she brings to her own classroom and her own home.
